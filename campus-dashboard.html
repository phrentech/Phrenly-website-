What you can and cannot see

The part worth reading before you decide anything else.

Available to your institution
  • ✓ Enrolment and active-user counts
  • ✓ Usage patterns by hour and by week
  • ✓ Which exercises get completed
  • ✓ Aggregate crisis-routing counts
  • ✓ Campus resource click-throughs
Never available, to anyone
  • ✕ Any conversation, in whole or in part
  • ✕ Which student used it, or when
  • ✕ Which students were flagged for risk
  • ✕ Mood entries or anything written in an exercise
  • ✕ Names, emails, or student IDs

Aggregation happens before data reaches this dashboard, and no per-student view exists to be granted. A student who believes they are being watched does not tell the truth at 1am — which would defeat the point of running the pilot at all.
