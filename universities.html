The question your committee will ask first

What happens when a student says they no longer want to be here

You should have a precise answer to this before you sign anything, from us or from anyone. Here is ours.

Phrenly monitors every conversation for risk — context and pattern, not just keywords.
When risk appears, it responds immediately, stays with the student, and surfaces crisis resources including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
It does not notify your staff, and it does not dispatch anyone. Phrenly is a confidential support tool, not a monitoring or duty-to-warn system.
Safety guidance never changes with tone or persona settings. It responds the same way every time.

We are direct about this because the alternative is worse. A tool that implies institutional escalation it cannot deliver is more dangerous than one that is clear about its limits. If your model requires escalation to campus staff, we should talk about whether Phrenly fits before you invest time in procurement.

For your review process

Privacy, access and compliance

  • Student privacyConversations are encrypted and are not shared with the institution. You receive aggregate usage data only — never individual student content.
  • Data processingWe sign a data processing agreement. Message content is processed by our AI providers to generate responses; student names and email addresses are never sent to them.
  • FERPAPhrenly does not access, ingest or store education records. We are happy to walk your counsel through the data flow.
  • DeletionStudents can delete their account and all associated data from inside the app, without contacting anyone.
  • AccessibilityAsk us for our current accessibility status before you shortlist — we would rather tell you plainly where we stand than have it surface during a security review.
  • RolloutStudents sign in with their university email. No IT integration required to start; SSO available on request.
