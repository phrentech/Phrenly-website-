How it works

From check-in to session, without the waiting-room nerves.

Everything a clinic waiting room should be, and a few things it can't: a friendly welcome, calming tools while you wait, and a session somewhere you feel at ease.

Step 1

Check in with Aria

Aria, our AI receptionist, welcomes you, checks you in and can book or move a session. Just talk to her.

Step 2

Wait somewhere calm

Try a guided breathing exercise, sit on the beach with a meditation, or talk things through with a companion until it's time.

Step 3

Meet your therapist

When they're ready, step through to the place you chose. Your therapist joins you there by video.

AI wellness companions

Someone to sit with, between sessions.

Your therapist can't be there every hour. On the days in between, sit down with an AI wellness companion in any of the calm places and talk things through. Choose who you sit with, how they speak to you and where. Try it here.

Just talk

No button to hold. Your companion listens, waits for you to finish your thought, and stops when you start speaking. You can type instead if you prefer.

Exercises when they help

Box Breathing, 4-7-8 Breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding, a body scan, kind words, or a meditation made for the place you're in. You choose whether to begin.

Honest about what it is

A companion is AI, not a therapist, and it supports your care without replacing it. If you may be in danger, it stops and shows crisis lines such as 988.

Why VR

Therapists have used VR for decades. The research is strong.

30 trials

Across 30 randomized trials (1,057 people), exposure therapy in VR worked as well as exposure in real life for anxiety disorders.

Carl et al., Journal of Anxiety Disorders, 2019
6 months

For PTSD, VR exposure therapy reduced symptoms more than control conditions, and the benefit held at 3- and 6-month follow-ups.

Deng et al., Journal of Affective Disorders, 2019
236 people

In a randomized trial with 236 people, time in virtual nature reduced anxiety and stress more than a placebo VR game.

International Journal of Clinical and Health Psychology, 2025

Today Phrenly VR offers calm places, guided breathing and meditation, AI wellness companions, and video sessions with your own therapist. Therapist-guided immersive exposure is on our roadmap.
