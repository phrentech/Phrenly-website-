How it works

From check-in to session, without the waiting-room nerves.

Everything a clinic waiting room should be, and a few things it can't: a friendly welcome, calming tools while you wait, and a session somewhere you feel at ease.

Step 1

Check in with Aria

Aria, our AI receptionist, welcomes you, checks you in and can book or move a session. Just talk to her.

Step 2

Wait somewhere calm

Try a guided breathing exercise, sit on the beach with a meditation, or simply listen to the forest until it's time.

Step 3

Meet your therapist

When they're ready, step through and sit down together in the place you chose. Your therapist joins by video.

The tour

Two minutes inside Phrenly VR.

Hear Aria's welcome, visit the calm places, and see what a session looks like.

The places

Choose where you feel most at ease.

Visit any of them on your own to breathe, meditate or just sit, or choose one for your session. Each has its own sounds: waves, birdsong, wind chimes over water.

Any device

Runs in the browser: Meta Quest, desktop and mobile. No app to install.

A real person

Aria is an AI receptionist. Your sessions are always with your therapist, over secure video.

Calm by design

Soft light, real places and natural sound, with guided breathing and meditations matched to each place.
