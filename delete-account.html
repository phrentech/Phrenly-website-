Delete your account and data

You can delete your Phrenly account at any time, without asking anyone's permission and without explaining why.

The fastest way — in the app

  1. Open Phrenly and sign in
  2. Tap the You tab (bottom right)
  3. Scroll to Account
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Deletion happens immediately. You do not need to contact us, and no one reviews or approves the request.

If you no longer have the app

Email hello@phrentech.com from the address you used to sign up, with the subject line Account deletion request. We will delete the account and confirm by reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.

We ask you to write from your sign-up address so that nobody can delete someone else's account.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account is removed:

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your conversation history
  • Mood check-ins and streaks
  • Exercise responses and progress
  • Anything Phrenly remembered about you between sessions
  • Data stored on your device

What we keep, and why

Two narrow exceptions, both required of us rather than chosen:

  • Billing records. If you ever subscribed, tax and accounting law requires us to retain transaction records. These contain the payment, not your conversations.
  • Safety records. Where a conversation triggered our crisis-detection system, a limited, de-identified record may be retained so we can review and improve how the app responds in those moments. It is not linked back to your account after deletion, is never sold, and is not readable by other users.

Nothing else survives deletion.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — those are held by Apple or Google, not by us. Cancel through your Apple or Google Play subscription settings before deleting, or you may continue to be billed.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you would like a copy of your data first, email us before you delete. Questions about any of this: hello@phrentech.com · See also our Privacy Policy.
