The question you’ll ask first

What happens if your client is in crisis between sessions

Before you recommend anything to a client, you need a precise answer to this. Here is ours.

Phrenly monitors every conversation for risk — context and pattern, not just keywords.
When risk appears, it responds immediately, stays with the client, and surfaces crisis resources for the country they are actually in — the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the US, and local lines and emergency numbers elsewhere.
It does not notify you, and it does not dispatch anyone. Phrenly is a confidential support tool, not a monitoring or duty-to-warn system — it never creates a reporting obligation you didn’t already have.
Safety guidance never changes with tone or persona settings. It responds the same way every time, and always points back toward human care — including you.

We are direct about this because the alternative is worse. A tool that implies clinical escalation it cannot deliver is more dangerous than one that is clear about its limits. Phrenly is not a substitute for treatment, a diagnosis, or an emergency service, and it never presents itself to your client as one.

For your due diligence

Privacy, boundaries and what Phrenly is not

  • Client-ownedYour client signs up directly and owns the account. Phrenly is their app, not a record you keep on them — so it sits outside your EHR and your HIPAA obligations as a provider.
  • You see only what’s sharedClinician sharing is opt-in and client-controlled. You receive an approved summary of skills and mood — never message content, and nothing if the client hasn’t turned it on.
  • Not a medical devicePhrenly is a wellness companion, not a diagnostic or treatment tool, and makes no clinical claims to your clients. It supplements care; it does not deliver it.
  • Encryption & AI handlingConversations are encrypted. Message content is processed by our AI providers to generate responses; names and email addresses are never sent to them.
  • DeletionClients can delete their account and all associated data from inside the app, at any time, without contacting anyone.
  • The relationship stays yoursPhrenly never positions itself as a replacement for you, and always points a client back toward human care.
