What happens when things get serious.

You should know this before you rely on an app, not after.

What Phrenly watches for

Every conversation is monitored for signs of crisis. Not just keywords, but context, tone and pattern over time.

Who it tells

Nobody.

Phrenly does not notify family, employers, schools or emergency services, and it does not dispatch anyone. It is a confidential support tool, not a monitoring system.

What it does

It responds immediately, stays in the conversation, and shows a crisis line and emergency number for the country you are actually in.

What never bends

Persona settings change how Phrenly talks day to day. They never change how it responds to risk.

What Phrenly is not

Not therapy, not a medical device, and not an emergency service. It supports people alongside professional care and points toward it.

In crisis? Call or text 988 (US) or your local emergency number.
